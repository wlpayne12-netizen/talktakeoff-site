How it works

No typing on a tailgate, and no spreadsheet back at the office.

Talk through the scope

Tap the mic and describe the job the way you'd tell your foreman: sizes, depths, counts, and whether it gets hauled off.

Check the line items

TalkTakeoff works out yards, tons, truck loads, and dump fees, then prices every line from your own price book. Fix anything with a tap.

Send the proposal

Your markup is built into the prices. Send a branded PDF by text or email before you leave the site.

Built for site work

Made by a contractor, for the work that general estimating apps don't handle well.

Takeoffs that do the math

Concrete and asphalt removal, building demo, excavation, undercut, import fill, stone, clearing, trees, grading, and erosion control.

Haul and disposal included

Loose yards, tons, and truck loads by volume and weight, with haul cost from your dump distance and the right tipping fees.

Your prices, your factors

Edit the price book and the swell, shrink, and density factors so every estimate matches how you actually bid.

Works with no signal

Everything runs on the phone. Bid from the middle of a cleared lot with no service.

Your data stays yours

Estimates live on your phone. There's no account to create, and nothing is uploaded anywhere unless you send it.

Try it on a sample job

Load a finished practice estimate and look around before you bid real work.
