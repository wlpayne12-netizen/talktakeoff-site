Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026 · Warren Payne LLC

This policy explains how the TalkTakeoff mobile app ("the app"), made by Warren Payne LLC ("we", "us"), handles information. In short: your estimates stay on your phone, we don't collect your data, and the app has no accounts, ads, or tracking.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter in the app stays in the app's storage on your phone and is not sent to us. That includes estimates, client names and contact details, job site addresses, notes, your price book, company information, and your logo. We don't operate a server that receives this information, and we can't see it.

If you delete the app, or clear its data in your phone's settings, this information is removed from your phone. Keep exported backups if you want copies.

Information you choose to share

The app sends information off your phone only when you do it. That happens when you send a proposal PDF, a spreadsheet (CSV) export, or a backup file through your phone's share options (email, text messages, cloud storage, and so on). Whatever app or service you pick then handles that file under its own privacy policy.

Microphone and speech recognition

When you tap the microphone, the app uses your phone's built-in speech recognition to turn what you say into text. The microphone is only used while dictation is on.

  • On iPhone and iPad, speech recognition is provided by Apple and may be processed on Apple's servers, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • On Android, speech recognition is provided by your phone's speech service (usually Google) and may be processed on its servers, under that provider's privacy policy.

We don't receive or store your audio. The resulting text stays in the app on your phone. You can type instead of dictating at any time.

Location

The app asks for your location only when you tap the location button on an estimate's job info. It's used once, to fill in the job site's coordinates. That text is saved with the estimate on your phone. The app doesn't track your location in the background.

Photos

If you choose a company logo, the app reads only the image you pick and stores a resized copy on your phone for your proposals.

What we don't do

  • We don't require an account or collect your name, email, or phone number through the app.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and we don't sell or share personal information.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

App stores and testing programs

Apple (the App Store and TestFlight) and Google (Google Play) may collect information such as downloads, crash reports, and device diagnostics under their own privacy policies. If you take part in a test program and send feedback, we receive what you choose to include, for example your comments and screenshots.

Contacting us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to respond to you. We don't add you to marketing lists without your permission.

Children

The app is a business tool for contractors and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your information, for example a future optional online backup or sharing between a company's phones, we'll update this policy and the effective date above before that feature is released.
