Support

We're here to help.

Email support@talktakeoff.com and we'll get back to you, usually within one business day. If something came out wrong after you dictated, include exactly what you said, word for word, and a screenshot. That lets us fix it fast.

Early access

TalkTakeoff is being field-tested by working crews before its public release. To join, email support@talktakeoff.com with your name, your company, and whether you use iPhone or Android.

Common questions

The microphone doesn't do anything.
Allow microphone access for TalkTakeoff in your phone's settings. On iPhone, go to Settings › TalkTakeoff, and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition. On Android, go to Settings › Apps › TalkTakeoff › Permissions › Microphone › Allow. You can also type the scope instead.

It misheard a number.
Fix the words in the text box before tapping Build line items. It also helps to say the unit every time ("square feet", "yards", "tons") and to pause between items.

Where are my estimates stored?
Only on your phone. There's no account, and nothing is uploaded. Back up regularly in Settings › Backup & transfer › Export backup, and save the file somewhere safe.

How do I move estimates to a new phone, or to a coworker?
Export a backup, send the file to the other phone, then open Settings › Backup & transfer › Import backup there.

How do I delete my data?
Delete individual estimates in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything on that phone. Since we don't keep a copy, there's nothing for us to delete on our end.

Are the built-in prices accurate for my area?
They're only starting points. Set your own unit costs in the Price Book before you bid. See our Terms of Use.
